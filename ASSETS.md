Read together with `CLAUDE.md` and `ROADMAP.md`.

Rules:
- Files in `assets/` are **source files**. Never edit them in place.
- Optimized derivatives (AVIF/WebP, multiple sizes) are generated by the media pipeline (step **S08**). Do not commit generated sizes.
- Files under `assets/_needs-review/` are **NOT approved for production**. Do not reference them anywhere.
- Anything not listed here does not exist yet — use a placeholder and log it in `docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.

## 5. Quality issues found — resolve before production

1. **`_needs-review/apelsin-500-mockup-FAKE-TEXT.png`** — the label text is not real. It contains nonsense strings and impossible dates ("с 2033 года", "2035-vildan buyon"). Do not publish. If a 500 ml product exists, request a real photo.

2. **`qulupnay-ananas-250-front.png`** — label mismatch: the artwork shows strawberry and pineapple, but the printed text reads *Гранатовый сок* (pomegranate). Confirm with BARFF which product this actually is before using.

3. **`barff-brand-banner.png`** — typo in `QO'SHIMCHA SHAKAR YO"Q` (wrong quote mark). Also the claims *100% NATURAL*, *QO'SHIMCHA SHAKAR YO'Q*, *KONSERVANT VA BO'YOQLARSIZ* are regulated product claims. Do not repeat them on the website until BARFF confirms them in writing.

4. **All bottle renders are 3D/AI-generated mockups.** Fine as thumbnails; risky at full-page or zoom size, where irregular label text becomes visible. Real studio photography is recommended for `/products/[slug]`.

5. **`apelsin-350-real-photo.jpg`** — real product, but 960×1280, 93 KB, phone-quality, and the label design differs from the render (darker red-orange). Confirm which design is currently in production; the site must show what the dealer actually receives.
